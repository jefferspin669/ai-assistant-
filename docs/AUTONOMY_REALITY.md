# Atlas automation: current execution contract

Atlas can create an assigned task in the background when the organization's subscription and autonomy policy authorize it. A queued task is not reported as complete until the scheduled `/api/autonomy/tick` worker persists the task and records its ID. At execution time the worker checks the requesting member's current status and role, tenant-scoped project and assignee, current plan and policy, time window, and kill switch. A failed job is recorded as failed and creates an owner notification. In manual mode, the owner can approve a proposed task; approval creates the task with its selected project and assignee.

Customer outreach through missed-call, waitlist, or invoice reminder jobs requires authority at send time and a real provider response. Incoming SMS records a requested appointment for owner review; Atlas does not book a fixed time just because the customer says “tomorrow.” A queued message, approval, or simulation is not evidence of delivery. Invoice recovery stops before its wait and payment outcome checks until delivery can be verified.

Autonomy action kinds other than task creation have no verified general-purpose background executor. Legacy queued autonomy actions fail visibly. An owner approval for a kind without a connected executor cannot send money or messages. The supported messaging jobs depend on configured Twilio or email credentials, Redis/BullMQ for generic background jobs, scheduled ticks, and provider webhooks. The owner must configure these in a deployed environment and validate delivery using a test account before relying on unattended customer contact.

The current persistence layer and scheduled ticks do not offer a transactionally claimed job across simultaneous server instances. Do not run concurrent cron replicas for this task executor until PostgreSQL row locking or equivalent atomic claims and unique task idempotency keys have been implemented and tested. Recheck quiet hours, provider retries, failures, two-business isolation, and manual owner stop on staging with real providers before calling unattended operations production ready.
