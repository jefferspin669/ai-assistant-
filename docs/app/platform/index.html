AI guardrails

Atlas won't confidently take the wrong action — actions are bounded, previewed, and audited.

🛡️

Never exceed configured approval limitsA $2,400 refund over a $500 limit is routed for approval, not auto-issued.

🛡️

Require explicit on-screen confirmation for destructive actionsDeleting a customer needs a typed/tapped confirmation and is audit-logged.

🛡️

Voice and AI actions honor the same permissions as the UI‘Show me payroll’ is refused for someone without payroll access.

🛡️

Ask before acting when intent is ambiguous‘Refund the customer’ with no amount → Atlas asks which order and how much.

🛡️

Preview & approve for broad actionsA company-wide broadcast shows a draft + audience count before sending.

🛡️

Everything important is recordedApprovals, overrides, deletions, and grants are written to the audit log.
